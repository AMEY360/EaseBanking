Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create an account?
To create an account, click on **Sign Up**, fill in your details, and verify via OTP.
What should I do if I forget my password?
Click **Forgot Password** on the login page. Enter your registered email/phone, and reset instructions will be sent to you.
Are my transactions secure?
Yes! We use **end-to-end encryption** and **multi-factor authentication** for security.
How can I check my account balance?
Log in and go to the **Dashboard**. Your balance and recent transactions will be shown there.
Is there a mobile app for EaseBanking?
No! But we are working to launch our mobile app to available on the **Google Play Store** and **Apple App Store**.
